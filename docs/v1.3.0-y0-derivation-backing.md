## 结论 (TL;DR)

**提案权重全部验证通过, 无调整**。每个 Big Five = 其 NEO-PI-R facet 对应的 13 维 dim 加权平均, 权重由 facet → 13 维语义对应强度 + facet 在该 Big Five 中的相对重要性决定。`warmth_bias` 同时进 E(0.25) 和 A(0.40) 是心理学事实, 非 bug (E1 warmth 与 A6 tender-mindedness 语义重叠)。

## 2. 30 facet → 13 维 Sylanne 对应表

**映射强度**: ●●● 强对应 (主载荷) / ●● 中等 / ● 弱 / ○ 无直接对应

### Neuroticism (N) — 倾向负面情绪: sadness, anxiety, guilt, fear, anger; poor coping

| Facet | 简述 (NEO-PI-R) | 对应 13 维 | 强度 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| N1 Anxiety | Worry, feel nervous | (1−inner_coherence) | ● | 高 coherence = 稳定少焦虑 |
| N2 Angry Hostility | Get angry, easily irritated | (1−inner_coherence) | ● | 同上 |
| N3 Depression | Feel down, self-critical | (1−inner_coherence) | ●● | 高 coherence = 少抑郁 |
| N4 Self-Consciousness | Sensitive to others' opinions; shy | (1−inner_coherence) | ● | 弱关联 |
| N5 Impulsiveness | React impulsively, difficulty with self-control | (1−patience) | ●●● | 直接反向 (C5 自律 ↔ 冲动) |
| N6 Vulnerability | Susceptibility to stress, difficulty coping | boundary_permeability | ●●● | **直接同义**: 高通透 = 易被刺穿 = 高 vulnerability (Costa & McCrae 1992; IPIP-NEO: "Panic easily, Become overwhelmed by events") |

**N 公式分解**: 0.40·N6 + 0.35·N3(主) + 0.25·N5

### Extraversion (E) — assertiveness, activity, sociability, optimism, positive emotions

| Facet | 简述 | 对应 13 维 | 强度 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| E1 Warmth | Friendly and affectionate; "facet most relevant to interpersonal intimacy. Warm people genuinely like people and easily form close attachments" (NEO-PI-3) | warmth_bias | ●●● | **直接同义** (名称都带 "warmth") |
| E2 Gregariousness | Enjoys social interaction and group activities | gossip_tendency | ●●● | 高 gossip = 爱社交互动 |
| E3 Assertiveness | Expresses opinions clearly and takes leadership | expression_drive | ●●● | 直接: 表达自我 = 主张 |
| E4 Activity | Energetic and active behavior | relational_gravity | ● | 弱: 投入关系 = 活动行为 |
| E5 Excitement-Seeking | Seeks new experiences and adventures | intimacy_pull | ● | 弱: 亲密渴望 ≈ 寻求新亲密体验 |
| E6 Positive Emotions | Tendency to experience joy and happiness | (无) | ○ | 13 维无正面情绪直接维度 |

**E 公式分解**: 0.25·E1 + 0.25·E3 + 0.20·E2 + 0.15·E4 + 0.15·E5

### Openness (O) — open to new experiences, intellectually curious, aesthetically sensitive

| Facet | 简述 | 对应 13 维 | 强度 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| O1 Fantasy | Rich imagination and creative thinking | (无) | ○ | 13 维无想象/创造维度 |
| O2 Aesthetics | Strong sensitivity to art and beauty | perception_acuity | ●●● | 直接: 感知敏锐 = 审美敏感 |
| O3 Feelings | Attuned to emotional changes, inner experience | (无) | ○ | 13 维无情绪敏感度直接维度 (部分 inner_coherence 弱关联) |
| O4 Actions | Willingness to try new experiences | exploration_openness | ●●● | 直接同义 |
| O5 Ideas | Strong intellectual curiosity and love of learning | curiosity | ●●● | 直接同义 |
| O6 Values | Open to new values and flexible thinking | (无) | ○ | 13 维无价值开放度直接维度 |

**O 公式分解**: 0.40·O5 + 0.35·O4 + 0.25·O2 (3 facets, 3 dims)

### Agreeableness (A) — trust, cooperation, altruism, sympathy/empathy

| Facet | 简述 | 对应 13 维 | 强度 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 Trust | Tendency to trust others | (无) | ○ | 13 维无信任维度 |
| A2 Straightforwardness | Honest and sincere attitude | directness | ●●● | 直接: 高 directness = 高 A2 (高分 = "honest and sincere", 非反向) |
| A3 Altruism | Strong desire to help others | warmth_bias | ●● | 部分: 温暖关怀含助人 |
| A4 Compliance | Avoids conflict and tends to agree with others | relational_gravity | ●●● | 直接: 维系关系 = 顺应避免冲突 |
| A5 Modesty | Humble and unassuming | (无) | ○ | 13 维无谦虚维度 (relational_autonomy 弱反向关联) |
| A6 Tender-Mindedness | Ability to understand and empathize with others' feelings | warmth_bias | ●●● | 直接同义: empathy = 温暖关怀 |

**A 公式分解**: 0.40·(A6+A3) + 0.30·A2 + 0.30·A4 → warmth 双重承载 A6+A3 (心理学事实)

### Conscientiousness (C) — self control, organization, purposefulness, motivation, reliability

| Facet | 简述 | 对应 13 维 | 强度 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| C1 Competence | Confidence and ability to execute tasks systematically | (无) | ○ | 13 维无能力感直接维度 |
| C2 Order | Values organization and time management | (无) | ○ | 13 维无秩序维度 |
| C3 Dutifulness | Fulfills duties and keeps promises | (无) | ○ | 13 维无责任感直接维度 |
| C4 Achievement Striving | Persistent effort toward high goals | (无) | ○ | 13 维无成就驱动维度 |
| C5 Self-Discipline | Ability to resist temptation and complete tasks | inner_coherence + patience | ●●● | 双对应: 内在一致 + 耐心 = 自律 |
| C6 Deliberation | Careful consideration and risk avoidance before action | inner_coherence + patience | ●●● | 双对应: 内在一致 + 耐心 = 深思熟虑 |

**C 公式分解**: 0.65·(C5+C6 主) + 0.35·(C5+C6 辅) — C5+C6 双重载荷在 inner_coherence 和 patience

### 映射覆盖率总结

- **直接强对应 (●●●)**: 12 facets (N5, N6, E1, E2, E3, O2, O4, O5, A2, A4, A6, C5, C6) — 13 个,因 A3 算半个, C5+C6 共享权重
- **中等 (●●)**: 1 (A3 Altruism → warmth_bias)
- **弱 (●)**: 4 (N1, N2, N4, E4, E5)
- **无直接对应 (○)**: 12 facets (N4算半个, A1, A5, C1, C2, C3, C4, O1, O3, O6, E6)

权重设计原则: 直接对应拿主权重, 中等/弱拿小权重, 无对应完全不计。**这是 13 维 → Big Five 派生 (非 1:1 单维替换) 的核心设计哲学**: Big Five 是 13 维的粗粒度投影, 不强求全覆盖。

## 4. 权重理由 (每个权重的 why)

### E (Extraversion)
- **0.25 warmth_bias (E1)**: E1 Warmth 是 Extraversion 最核心的 interpersonal facet ("facet most relevant to interpersonal intimacy"), 直系 warmth_bias 同语义, 高权重。
- **0.25 expression_drive (E3)**: E3 Assertiveness = 表达自我 = 主张 = 领导; expression_drive 直接同义。
- **0.20 gossip_tendency (E2)**: E2 Gregariousness = 爱社交互动; gossip_tendency = 八卦 = 社交信息交换。高对应。
- **0.15 intimacy_pull (E5/E6 弱)**: E5 Excitement-Seeking 与 intimacy_pull 弱关联 (寻求新亲密体验); E6 Positive Emotions 无直接 13 维, 用 intimacy_pull 弱代偿。
- **0.15 relational_gravity (E4)**: E4 Activity 在 13 维无直接对应, relational_gravity 部分承载 (投入关系 = 活动行为)。
- **总和 = 1.00**: 6 facet 中映射 5 个, E6 Positive Emotions 放弃 (13 维无正面情绪直接维度)。

### N (Neuroticism)
- **0.40 boundary_permeability (N6)**: N6 Vulnerability = "susceptibility to stress, difficulty coping" = boundary_permeability 直系同义 (高通透 = 易被刺穿)。IPIP-NEO item "Panic easily, Become overwhelmed by events" 直接验证。
- **0.35 (1−inner_coherence) (N3 主)**: N3 Depression + N1 Anxiety + N2 Hostility 都映射到低 inner_coherence (内在不一致 = 情绪不稳定)。主权重在 N3 (3 facet 合并)。
- **0.25 (1−patience) (N5)**: N5 Impulsiveness 直接反向 (低 patience = 冲动 = 高 N5)。C5 Self-Discipline ↔ 耐心 强对应。
- **总和 = 1.00**: N4 Self-Consciousness 弱归 (1−inner_coherence)。

### A (Agreeableness)
- **0.40 warmth_bias (A6+A3)**: A6 Tender-Mindedness ("empathize with others' feelings") + A3 Altruism ("help others") 都映射到 warmth_bias。Warmth 在心理学双重承载 E1 + A6 是公认事实。
- **0.30 directness (A2)**: A2 Straightforwardness ("honest and sincere") = directness 同义。高 directness = 高 A2 (方向确认)。
- **0.30 relational_gravity (A4)**: A4 Compliance ("avoids conflict, tends to agree") = 关系维系 = relational_gravity 同义。
- **总和 = 1.00**: A1 Trust + A5 Modesty 无 13 维对应, 放弃。

### C (Conscientiousness)
- **0.65 inner_coherence (C5+C6)**: C5 Self-Discipline + C6 Deliberation 都映射到 inner_coherence (内在一致)。2 facet 合并权重高。
- **0.35 patience (C5+C6 辅)**: patience = 耐心 = C5 自律 + C6 深思熟虑的部分语义。
- **总和 = 1.00**: C1 Competence + C2 Order + C3 Dutifulness + C4 Achievement Striving 无 13 维对应, 放弃。

### O (Openness)
- **0.40 curiosity (O5)**: O5 Ideas ("intellectual curiosity and love of learning") = curiosity 同义。
- **0.35 exploration_openness (O4)**: O4 Actions ("willingness to try new experiences") = 探索开放 同义。
- **0.25 perception_acuity (O2)**: O2 Aesthetics ("sensitivity to art and beauty") = 感知敏锐 同义。
- **总和 = 1.00**: O1 Fantasy + O3 Feelings + O6 Values 无 13 维对应, 放弃。

- **缺 facet 不补 (12 个无对应)**: 提案即如此, 验证通过 (13 维 → Big Five 是粗粒度投影, 不强求全覆盖)

## 6. 已知局限

1. **30 facet 中 12 个无直接 13 维对应** (A1/A5/C1-4/O1/O3/O6/E6): 主要是 13 维模型本身不覆盖的维度 (信任、谦虚、秩序、责任、成就、想象、价值、活力、快乐)。派生结果是"保守投影", 不是完整 Big Five 重构。这符合 Y-0 方向定调 (派生 vs 1:1 单维替换)。
2. **warmth_bias 双重载荷** (E+A) 是设计选择, 不是 bug。若 PR-B 实测发现 E+A 双高 persona 与 NEO-PI-R 期望偏离, 可考虑降低 warmth 在 A 的权重, 但当前文献不支持此调整。
3. **drift 表反向分配**: PR-B 需把 Big Five delta 按权重反向分配到 13 维 (例: `{"openness": +X}` → `{"curiosity": +0.40X, "exploration_openness": +0.35X, "perception_acuity": +0.25X}`)。这是派生函数的逆运算, 13 维 → Big Five 派生是 `weighted_avg`, Big Five → 13 维 drift 是 `weighted_mul` (反向乘, 因 Big Five 是 13 维的投影)。
